🇵🇰 Welcome to NADRA Official Portal

NADRA (National Database and Registration Authority) is the premier government agency of Pakistan responsible for secure identity management, digital verification, and national registration.

Established in 1998, NADRA has been at the forefront of Pakistan's digital transformation, providing state-of-the-art identity solutions to over 220 million citizens.

🔒 Over 220 Million identities managed • 100% secure • 24/7 availability
